`★ Insight ─────────────────────────────────────`
- The sharpest defect here isn't a wrong API call — every signature the plan cites is real. It's a **data-flow gap**: the plan treats "the normalized CSVs exist" as sufficient, but the economics adapter is wired to a loader that only *produces* those CSVs from raw XLSX and never *consumes* them. Plans that verify existence-of-outputs but not direction-of-reads routinely hide re-download/re-write side effects like this.
- The `onshore_model_mismatch` finding shows why "carry the caveat through" is not the same as "compute the caveat correctly": the flag is only as trustworthy as the `environment` field feeding `_is_onshore`, and that field is absent from the live schema — so a caveat meant to protect offshore fields silently switches off for exactly those fields.
`─────────────────────────────────────────────────`

Bottom line: two correctness-critical gaps (economics loader source; environment classification for the mismatch caveat) block APPROVE. Both are fixable at plan stage by naming a normalized-CSV loader (or reusing raw with an explicit offline guard) and a curated field→environment map, or by scoping economics to Ayoluengo only.
