1. **Different Assumptions:** The Excel data may use different:
   - Cash flow timing and amounts
   - Tax considerations
   - Operating expense structures
   - Salvage values

2. **Different Scenarios:** Excel may contain:
   - Multiple well configurations
   - Various oil price assumptions
   - Different production profiles
   - Optimistic vs. conservative cases

3. **Data Interpretation Issues:** 
   - Excel values might include gross vs. net calculations
   - Different project scopes or phases
   - Present value vs. NPV calculations
