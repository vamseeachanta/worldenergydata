BSEE lease ingest

Scope: issue 959. The ingest will load the published fixed Lease Data layout and delimited Lease Owner, Lease Owner with Designated Operator, and Company (All) layouts. Source column aliases, positions, dates and percentage types will be retained in a lease-specific schema. Verified download layouts will govern parsing ahead of the conceptual dictionary; ASSIGNMENT_PCT will remain percentage points, never a fraction.

New files will live in data/modules/bsee/leases/, package modules lease_ingest.py and lease_fetch.py, scripts/bsee/fetch_leases.py, tests/fixtures/bsee/leases/ and lease-specific unit tests. Shared BSEE files, registry configuration and panels will remain outside scope.

Owner rows will retain assignment identity, group, status, dates and percentage. Only source status C will contribute to current-interest arrays. Historical rows will remain in the owner table. Per-lease approval_date and gwi_pct will be arrays aligned to current owner records and explicit owner identifiers/groups in a supplemental structured table; percentages will never be added across groups. Operator numbers will resolve through Company (All). Owner and operator rows will aggregate separately, avoiding a many-to-many join; designation dates will remain in the operator table. Missing names and missing source rows will carry explicit reasons.

The extract will select registry fields with lifecycle_poster enabled and Lower Tertiary in the play description: 20 leases across 10 fields. Every extracted lease will be checked with by_lease; unknown leases and absent source data will be reported separately. A supplemental panel-values table will preserve actual and expected expiration dates distinctly.

Sequential HTTPS requests will use timeouts, a descriptive User-Agent and at most one retry. Access refusals will stop downloads. Raw files will remain in environment-configured external storage. Every saved source and derived artifact will carry a digest and be read back. Terms will reflect the BSEE disclaimer without inventing an explicit license grant.

Offline recorded fixtures will precede implementation. Tests will cover exact layouts, malformed data, multiple owners, historical exclusion, dates, registry variants and unknowns, fetch refusal/retry boundaries and provenance integrity. Formatting, lint and type checks will target the touched code. A frozen review packet will pin plan and code reviews.

Overlap: open PR 1155 adds atlas-specific BSEE files, with no planned lease-path collision. PRs 1156, 1154, 1153, 1150 and 1136 do not modify the selected lease paths. Shared README, DATA_DICTIONARY.md, schema.yaml, _metadata.json and freshness scorecard integration will remain deferred, along with panel wiring.
