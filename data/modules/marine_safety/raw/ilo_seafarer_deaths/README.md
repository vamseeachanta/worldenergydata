## Data Content

This report provides:

- **Global Context:** International maritime labor standards
- **Seafarer Demographics:** Employment numbers by region and vessel type
- **Safety Standards:** ILO Maritime Labour Convention (MLC, 2006) compliance
- **Working Conditions:** Hours of work, rest periods, health protections
- **Recommendations:** Policy guidance for maritime safety improvement
