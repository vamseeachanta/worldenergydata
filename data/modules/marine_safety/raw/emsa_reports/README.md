## Data Content

These annual reports provide comprehensive analysis of:

- **Marine Casualties:** Ship losses, collisions, groundings, fires
- **Marine Incidents:** Near-misses, equipment failures, pollution events
- **Vessel Types:** All commercial vessel categories
- **Geographic Scope:** European waters (EU/EEA member states)
- **Data Source:** European Marine Casualty Information Platform (EMCIP)
- **Statistical Analysis:** Trends, vessel types, casualty categories, human factors
