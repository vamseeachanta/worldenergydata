## Data Content

Paris MOU annual reports provide:

- **Inspection Statistics:** Number of inspections conducted in European/North Atlantic ports
- **Deficiency Categories:**
  - Safety management systems
  - Life-saving appliances
  - Fire safety measures
  - Navigation equipment
  - Crew competency and certification
  - Working/living conditions
- **Detention Data:** Vessels detained for serious deficiencies
- **Flag State Performance:** Country-level compliance rankings
- **Target Factor System:** Risk-based ship selection for inspection
- **Regional Coverage:** 27 European maritime authorities + Canada

## Paris MOU Performance Lists

The annual report includes three performance lists:

1. **White List:** Low-risk flag states (high safety compliance)
2. **Grey List:** Medium-risk flag states
3. **Black List:** High-risk flag states (poor safety record)

These lists inform target factor calculations for ship inspections.
