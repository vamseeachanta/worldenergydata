Limits

  • Published abandoned, removed and out meanings supplement the existing inactive-status rule. Dictionary-undefined codes are excluded. Cancelled, combined and proposed statuses can remain under this exclusion rule; selection does not establish installation or present operation. Identical permit engineering joins collapse; conflicting values reject.
  • Geometry uses published coordinates and spherical distances; no datum transformation is performed.
  • Maximum depth cannot establish minimum depth; absent burial design is not exposed design.
  • Nominal size can be an OD proxy. Hoop sizing includes no design factor, corrosion allowance, tolerance or collapse check.
  • Local schedule lookup does not establish installed wall. Public outputs require source rights and edition qualification.
  • NDBC fits are independent marginal extrapolations. Retained record span does not establish coverage, coincident design conditions or site transfer.
  • Nearest sediment texture does not establish route geotechnical properties. Sample distance is retained; friction is a labelled assumption.
  • Unknown coating, concrete and contents properties require an evidenced basis before use in stability calculations.
  • Current and wave direction defaults do not establish seabed storm conditions.
