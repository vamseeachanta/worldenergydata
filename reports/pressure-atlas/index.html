Methodology

Offshore figures are public field disclosures (OGJ, OTC/JPT, OffshoreMag, operator releases) — disclosed reservoir (pore/BHP) pressures, distinct from the wellhead equipment rating; undisclosed values render —. Onshore figures come from state well databases (Kansas KGS, Oklahoma OCC, Texas RRC) screened by the worldenergydata underpressured_screen BHP gas-column estimate. Onshore is a depleted-era proxy — it measures economic operability at extreme-low bottomhole pressure in mature gas areas, not virgin sub-hydrostatic reservoirs. Hydrostatic reference: 0.433 psi/ft. Every number on this page traces to the committed _pressure.json.
