Drilling & Completion Days — Verification

Field- and well-level reconciliation of the live worldenergydata (WED) BSEE extract against the frozen WO Article, end of 2025 benchmark (World Oil Lower-Tertiary series, Table 1 — “Project and well metrics, BSEE data derived summary through 2025”). A living QA/QC surface: flagged rows stay open until each difference is resolved.
← back to the completion-days report

How to read this

Metric. D&C days = drilling + completion days (spud→total-depth plus total-depth→final-completion). Wellbores = unique API well numbers (the WO article's convention). The main report breaks drilling and completion out separately in its per-field stat cards; this page uses the combined D&C total so it is directly comparable to the WO benchmark.
Like-for-like. The headline reconciliation is the 9 developments both sources carry (WED 19,445 vs WO 19,940 D&C days). WED-only Big Foot and WO-only Buckskin are shown as separate, non-comparable line items; the raw grand totals (22,478 vs 21,944) span different field sets, so a near-match there is coincidental offsetting, not agreement.
V30 vs V50. Drilling/completion days and wellbore counts are derived from BSEE Well Activity Reports and are identical under V30 and the V50 latest-OGOR rerun — V50 extends only the production window (economics), not these day counts. So this reconciliation is version-independent.

Provenance

WED figures are computed deterministically from the frozen workbook drilling_and_completion_days.xlsx (BSEE Well Activity Reports, V30 methodology). WO-Article figures are transcribed verbatim from World Oil Lower-Tertiary Table 1 (BSEE-derived summary thru Nov 2025) and are held fixed as an external benchmark.
