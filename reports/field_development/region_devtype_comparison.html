Coverage & honesty

This universe is the curated production_facilities.csv (836 standalone facilities). Two limits worth stating plainly:

  • Subsea tiebacks are under-counted. A tieback is not a standalone facility — it hangs off a host — so only 266 wet-tree hulls appear here, of which just the 54 that the source lists as their own line are tiebacks. Where we do have block-level ground truth (the GoM SubseaIQ–BSEE crosswalk, 75 typed fields), subsea tiebacks are 10 of them — the true wet-tree footprint is larger than a facility census shows.
  • The typed/untyped split is honest per region. 56 of 836 facilities (7%) are non-tree hulls (FSO/FSU, MOPU, artificial island) and are held out of the dry-vs-wet ratios rather than forced into one bucket. 98 facilities lack a recorded water depth and are excluded from the depth chart only.
Sources: data/modules/offshore_assets/curated/production_facilities.csv (836 rows, HOST_TYPE → concept → tree_type) · subseaiq_bsee_block_crosswalk.csv (115 matched GoM rows, supplementary subsea-tieback prevalence only).
