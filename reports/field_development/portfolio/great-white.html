Engineering rationale

Great White is the anchor field of the Perdido regional hub; the truss spar sits directly on Alaminos Canyon 857 over the field in ~2438 m of water, chosen as a stable deep-draft floating host that supports direct vertical access drilling and dual-gradient subsea boosting in this remote ultra-deepwater frontier. Because the low-energy Lower Tertiary Wilcox reservoirs lack the pressure to flow unaided to surface from such depth, all wells are subsea wet trees commingled into seabed caissons with electrical submersible pumps that boost fluids up the spar risers. The spar gathers Great White plus the nearby Tobago and Silvertip fields within a ~16 km radius, amortizing one host across multiple small Lower Tertiary accumulations. Notable: Hosted by the Perdido spar, the world's deepest spar/production platform at installation.
