Engineering rationale

The initial development uses high-pressure subsea technology with production routed to a floating processing system. Later-phase producers and the nearby southern discovery are separate scopes. The startup oil-deliverability target is distinct from host nameplate capacity.

Architecture

Initial-phase subsea producers feed the floating production system. The semisubmersible classification follows the committed lifecycle research; the opened startup report calls the host an FPS. Additional-phase and southern-discovery tiebacks are excluded from the recorded well count.
