Engineering rationale

The reported arrangement combines drilling, workover and production on an extended tension-leg platform. Dry trees and top-tensioned risers provide direct well access from the facility. This is a description of the reported selection, not a fresh comparison of host concepts.

Architecture

Dry-tree wellheads on the platform connect through top-tensioned risers; processed fluids leave through export infrastructure. No subsea wet-tree layout is implied.

Well coordinates, manifold counts and interconnection lengths are required inputs for a detailed layout. Layout: NOT_EVALUATED; no assumed geometry is drawn.
