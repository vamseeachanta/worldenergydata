The default view shows the 120 concept-matched Gulf of Mexico fields. Selecting a country loads the global catalog — 2,149 fields across 84 countries; the GoM catalog carries 333 entries (of BSEE's full 1,390-field atlas; Lower Tertiary = 111 fields, ~36% of GoM oil). Density (per field): rich = life-cycle hub + economics · sample = concept data · roadmap = name/block only. Country badges (in the Country list) reflect national data-module depth, not per-field detail. GoM play attribution beyond the concept-matched set is pending the BSEE sands-atlas ingest (#955) — use the (unattributed) play filter to see those fields.
