Method and sources

Arps rate and cumulative equations: ResInsight Decline Curve Analysis (accessed 2026). Exact monthly volumes are integrated to the rate limit, including partial months. Time-zero CAPEX and month-end net operating cash are discounted through FDAS; numpy-financial 1.0.0, npv function and Notes. Percentiles use NumPy 2.2 linear percentiles; ranks use SciPy 1.15.2 Spearman correlation.

Limits and way forward

Independent priors; empirical decline with no pressure, facility constraints, tax, royalties, fixed operating costs or terminal value. Synthetic inputs do not establish public-field reserve distributions. Calibrated public production data, documented priors and a governing acceptance criterion are required before field screening. No reserve classification is established. Charts are static vectors.
