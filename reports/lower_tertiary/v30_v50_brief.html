Re-running the V30 methodology on raw BSEE OGOR reproduces the frozen golden_baseline_v30.yml within ±0.1% on production and ±1% on NPV for every matched field. 3 · Code reconciliation — which code is right

The refresh surfaced two analysis lanes that disagreed. We picked the canonical one and unified them.

Decision — one engine, two vintages.
  • Single engine. V30 and V50 are the same function with different arguments (end_date, first_oil_overrides); no forked methodology. Default arguments reproduce V30 byte-for-byte.
  • Cascade Chinook first-oil correction adopted for V50. Raw OGOR shows first production in 2012-09; the golden V30 carried 2014-01 — a verified error. V50 uses the corrected date (its oil now matches the independent latest-data run); V30 stays frozen so it keeps matching Roy's sanctioned workbook.
  • Jack St Malo D&C timing documented, not silently "fixed." The raw-OGOR monthly allocation is fully reproducible from public source; the ~7.3% offset vs the workbook is recorded as expected.

4 · Assumptions audit the basis under every number

V50 reuses the V30 assumptions verbatim (same workbook) — that is what makes the comparison a clean data-only diff. But the basis itself has open items worth a decision, separate from the data refresh:

What the engine actually uses. Of the 39 assumptions in lease_assumptions.xlsx, the engine reads only ~11 — all cost/price/rate inputs (discount 10%, royalty 18.75%, opex $4–6/bbl, host capex, rig dayrates, …). The NPV is pre-tax: corporate tax (21%), depreciation, 70% IDC, NOL carry-forward, severance, and the royalty-relief threshold (50 MMbbl) are defined in the workbook but never applied — even though an after-tax reference document sits beside it.

5 · Recommendation

  • Tier 1 — data refresh (done, sanctioned): V50. Same basis → deltas reflect data only.
  • Tier 2 — basis refresh (needs your sign-off): the price decks are current to 2026-05, but the cost decks (rig dayrates, opex, capex) and the 10% / 18.75% rates are original 2014-era figures. Rig dayrates are the most likely stale.
  • Tier 3 — model extension: wire in the after-tax block + royalty relief the workbook already specifies, and consolidate to one assumptions deck (retire/relabel the $75 YAML).

Keep V50 on the V30 basis for this deliverable; treat Tier 2/3 as separate, explicitly-sanctioned revisions because they change the answer for non-data reasons.
