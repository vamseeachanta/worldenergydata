Well calendar cycle time

The former drilling learning-curve page now describes elapsed calendar time. Among 217 named development wellbores in the saved workbook, 186 have both dates in chronological order. Their median spud-to-TD interval is 47.5 calendar days and their sum is 35,771.0 calendar days. The sum adds wellbore intervals; it can count overlapping dates and is not a measure of rig occupancy.

Definition and population

Interval = total-depth date minus spud date, in elapsed calendar days, with no inclusive-day addition. A same-day interval is zero. 31 rows have missing or invalid dates; 0 have TD before spud. Those rows remain in the download with blank intervals and are excluded from interval statistics. Rows without a lease name (blank and totals rows) are excluded. No cutoff year, completion status, depth or duration threshold selects this date-qualified population.

The benchmark uses a different endpoint: spud_to_completion_calendar_days = first completion marker minus spud; completion_days = first oil minus first completion marker. Both are calendar components including suspension and waiting, rather than active drilling time.

Historical 184 versus 211 comparison

Issues #1077 and #1062 (2026) record the former 184-wellbore / 11,124-day claim and the article's 211-well / 21,944-day claim. The former selected positive legacy workbook durations; the current count above instead requires valid spud/TD dates. The article's population has 27 more entries, but different selection rules and day definitions prevent interpreting this as 27 omitted drilling records. Exact paired membership and the article's operational day basis are NOT_EVALUATED: the saved workbook alone does not establish either. The producing-well benchmark is another population, restricted to producing wells in its configured spud window. Neither historical total is used to infer rig-time performance here.

Worked example

Synthetic inputs: spud 2020-01-01, TD 2020-01-08, first completion 2020-03-01, first oil 2020-06-09. Spud to TD = 7 calendar days. Spud to completion = 31 + 29 = 60 calendar days (leap year). Completion to first oil = 31 + 30 + 31 + 8 = 100 calendar days. The benchmark decomposition is 60 + 100 = 160 calendar days from spud to first oil. None establishes active rig time.
