# World Oil April 2026 — per-well D&C days listing

One row per wellbore behind the field-level D&C reconciliation matrix in
[the validation page, section 3](wo-april-2026-validation.html) — the
"listing" requested in the QA/QC review so each development's total can
be audited bore by bore against the article's Table 1.

## Definitions and basis

- **Source**: canonical extractor output `drilling_and_completion_days_v21_kc.csv` (V50 = wed basis, BSEE WAR vintage 2026-02-19): 253 wellbores across 26 leases, 11 developments.
- **Drilling days** = rig-days from spud to total depth (TD). **Completion days** = rig-days after TD — this includes later recompletion and well-servicing rig time, the accounting behaviour tracked in [#846](https://github.com/vamseeachanta/worldenergydata/issues/846).
- **Producer** markers come from the OGOR-A well benchmark (56 producing wells across the seven core LT fields); its calendar-day columns are never used here.
- **Sidetrack** bores are identified by a non-zero API12 suffix (last two digits).
