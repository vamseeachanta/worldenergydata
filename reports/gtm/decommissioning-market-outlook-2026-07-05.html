6 · Commercial read-through

  • Large, aging pipeline: 7,405 eligible wells, 3,190 already idle, 2,217 idle ≥10 years — a standing backlog independent of new end-of-life additions.
  • Durable multi-year demand: several hundred P&A wells/year at base pace — a sustained market for well-plugging, rig/vessel, casing-cut and conductor-removal contractors.
  • Capacity-gap signal: observed 2020-2024 P&A (358/yr) runs below the base-case requirement — a backlog-clearing gap favoring decom-capable vendors.
