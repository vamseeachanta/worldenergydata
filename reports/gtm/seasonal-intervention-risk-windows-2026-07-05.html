Data vs assumptions — hard separation. All monthly/seasonal/activity counts are measured from local BSEE WAR-derived CSVs. The only non-data input is the Atlantic hurricane-season calendar (Jun 1–Nov 30 season, Aug–Oct peak), which is public NOAA/NHC climatology, used purely to overlay the measured counts. This is a descriptive analysis — the local extracts are small sample CSVs (N=177), so no statistical-significance claim is made.
Scope-down (per #426). The assembled data/modules/hse/hse_incidents.db is a 0-byte stub and no raw BSEE INCINV file (mv_acc_investigations.txt) is present locally, so the HSE-incident-rate-by-season cut and the #416 Bonferroni-significance recommendation cannot be produced from local data and are deferred. This report delivers the WAR operations-timing × hurricane-season overlay only.

4 · Operational decision-support read-through

Hurricane-season window (assumption — public): NOAA National Hurricane Center — official Atlantic hurricane season (Jun 1-Nov 30) and climatological peak (mid-Aug to mid-Oct). Public knowledge.

Where intervention scope is schedulable, front-loading discretionary work into the Dec–May off-season (and avoiding the Aug–Oct peak) reduces exposure to storm-driven deferral, evacuation, and weather-window loss. This is a directional, descriptive recommendation; the local sample (N=177) is too small to attach a quantified HSE-rate delta or a significance test — that awaits the INCINV/HSE data and the #403 metocean overlay.
