Well & drilling engineering

Julia's development is 9 wellbores (4 producing) drilled to ~30,000 ft MD beneath 7,335 ft of water, 1,687 total rig-days (≈ $1.35B of MODU time at $0.8M/day). Campaign 2008-02-17 → 2019-11-17. Drilling data is the real BSEE/FDAS V30 per-well record; methodology mirrors aceengineercode ong_field_development.

Methodology: BSEE OGOR-A monthly oil × EIA WTI monthly − royalty (18.75% federal) − variable OPEX ($6/bbl) − fixed OPEX ($75M/yr) − D&C (drilling+completion days × $0.8M/day MODU) − facilities (SURF $250M/well + booster pump $275M + water-injection facility $100M; tieback15 → no host CAPEX). NPV trimmed to first/last non-zero month, discounted 10% annual (monthly compounded); MIRR Excel-style (reinvest 8% / finance 6%). Vintages: V30 = OGOR+WTI through 2025-05 (golden-baseline window, field/block figures authoritative); Latest = OGOR+WTI through Nov 2025 (last complete OGOR month; Dec 2025 partial, excluded). Per-well NPV (≈) allocates shared CAPEX & fixed OPEX by production share and is indicative, not a re-derivation of the field NPV.
