Avoidable failure modes → planning controls

  • Foundering / capsizing / sinking: verify storm-category survivability before choosing stay/relocate/refuge.
  • Flooding and water ingress: close hatch, door, ventilator, and access-cover weak points before surge and wave exposure.
  • Severe-weather exposure: use track proximity and forecast category thresholds to leave early enough.
  • Mooring operation exposure: reduce last-minute deck work through pre-planned line layout and crew readiness.

Control rows excluded from incident counts include NI001, NI002, NI003, NI004, NI005, NI006, NI007, NI008, NI009, NI010. NI010 is a positive/control row: weather routing helped avoid severe storm; it is not counted as a loss event.

Port / refuge decision tree

This is the decision lever from the DOCX: convert site and vessel constraints into a category-by-category action rule before the hurricane season.

1. Pre-season engineering check Bollard, fender, and mooring-line capacity checks; bulkhead geometry; vessel particulars; survivability by storm category.
2. Forecast trigger Track proximity, surge window, changing wind/current directions, port closure timing, and crew availability.
3. Action before lock-in Stay with engineered mooring plan, shift berth, move to refuge, or evacuate before conditions make movement unsafe.
