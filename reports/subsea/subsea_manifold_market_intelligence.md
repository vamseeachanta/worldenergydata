## Key findings

- **Consolidation around three SPS majors.** The integrated subsea production system (manifolds + trees + controls) market is dominated by **TechnipFMC**, **SLB OneSubsea** (the SLB 70% / Aker Solutions 20% / Subsea7 10% JV, closed Oct 2023), and **Baker Hughes**. Aker Solutions and Subsea7 now reach the manifold-OEM market primarily *through* the OneSubsea JV.
- **EPCI vs OEM split.** **McDermott** and **Subsea7** are tier-1 EPCI/SURF players that fabricate and install manifolds but integrate third-party SPS hardware rather than manufacturing the valve/control internals.
- **Independents fill custom / fast-track demand.** **Trendsetter Engineering** (niche, private) and **Dril-Quip/Innovex** (tier-2) supply fit-for-purpose manifolds and connection systems; Trendsetter acquired Innovex's subsea-tree line in 2025.
- **Adjacencies, not manifold OEMs.** **ABB** (subsea power & automation) and **Halliburton** (subsea completions & intervention) appear in some 'subsea manifold market' lists but do **not** manufacture manifolds — they supply enabling subsystems.
- **Live M&A to watch.** The **Saipem–Subsea7 merger ('Saipem7')** was signed in July 2025 and remains pending regulatory clearance (UK CMA) as of June 2026.
