## 💡 Major Findings

### Capital Structure
- **$1.8B = Total Phase 1 cost** (not per partner)
- Partners share by working interest %
- Phase 2: $350M additional
- Total new partner capital: $2.4B

### IRR Results
- **Navitas:** 29% (base), 37% (realistic with $85 oil)
- **Beacon:** 19% (entered later, paid more)
- **All-in project:** 4.5% (society's return over 22 years)
- **Original partners:** -100% (complete loss)

### Well Analysis
- **All 4 producing wells:** Drilled 2022-2024 (NEW)
- **6 historical wells:** Plugged & abandoned
- **Real subsidy:** $650M-1.3B in geological data
- **True economic IRR:** 40-50% (counting data subsidy)

### Value Transfer vs. Creation
- **Value transferred:** $8.9B (from old to new partners)
- **Value created:** $6.9B (at sub-optimal 4.5% return)
- **Paradox:** Individual success ≠ societal success
