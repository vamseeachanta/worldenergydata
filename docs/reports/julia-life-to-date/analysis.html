2 Introduction

The analysis calculates a field-level pre-tax assumption scenario from retained public production filings. The demonstration summary identifies Julia as the field; it is used only for field selection [I2].

The scope ends at the latest observed field-production month. Decline extrapolation, booked-reserve valuation, full-cycle economics and actual expenditure reconciliation are excluded.

3 Design Data

The input basis comprises field-coded production, well/completion identifiers and field identifiers from public federal filings. The retained rows and their source archive digests are listed in the accompanying manifest.

3.1 Site or field data

Julia; leases G20351, G20361, G25251; field code WR627; block WR 584. Observed production spans 2016-03 to 2026-06, with 124 monthly observations. Earlier retained production records contain zero oil production.

3.2 Structure or equipment data

Well and completion filings support lease identity. Equipment design and mechanical capacities are not evaluated because the calculation uses field-level production and an assumed capital total.

3.3 Environmental data

Not applicable: no environmental loads or physical equipment response are calculated.

3.4 Vessel data

Not applicable: no vessel operation or vessel-dependent cost is modelled.

3.5 Criteria

Input digests, complete monthly coverage, numerical summary agreement and repeatable output bytes are required by the retained criteria record [I3]. The discounted cashflow balance is compared with zero; that arithmetic comparator does not qualify an investment decision.

4 Analysis Methodology

Production is summed by month across all retained field-coded completions. Monetary quantities are calculated using the monthly financial and field-summary functions in worldenergydata [I1]; no production forecast is used.

Gross revenue is oil volume multiplied by the assumed oil price plus gas volume multiplied by the assumed gas price. Royalty is gross revenue multiplied by the assumed royalty fraction. Operating expenditure is energy-equivalent production multiplied by the assumed unit operating cost.

Pre-tax operating cashflow is gross revenue less royalty and operating expenditure. Gas energy-equivalence follows the existing module convention of 6.0 Mcf/boe; gas in BCF is the reported Mcf total divided by one million.

Operating cashflow is discounted by (1 + annual discount rate)elapsed months / 12, with the first observed oil month as the origin. The life-to-date balance is discounted operating cashflow less assumed capital charged at that origin.

The computational comparator is independent summation and cashflow arithmetic from retained rows. Digest equality and repeatable builds establish traceability and reproduction; actual sales, expenditure and fiscal records are needed to establish financial accuracy.

6 Key Findings

The findings apply to the observed-production assumption case and its stated data window.

6.1 Observed production

The retained field-coded rows sum to 93.8 MMbbl oil and 10.3 BCF gas through 2026-06. These are reported production quantities, not recoverable reserves.

6.2 Economic balance

The discounted balance is -2.2 USD billion against zero for the assumed price, cost and fiscal case. Actual field profitability and full-cycle value are not established.

    8 Recommendations

    The next economic analysis requires an observed fiscal and expenditure basis. The following actions resolve the material assumptions.

    1. Reconcile prices, operating and capital expenditure, royalty payments and relief with cited public records before qualifying actual financial performance; issue 651 retains the assumption and model-extension scope.
    2. Refresh the production snapshot before a later analysis window is used.
    3. Qualify recoverable reserves under issue 973 before calculating a full-cycle value.
