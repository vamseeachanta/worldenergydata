# Detail behind the life and water-depth sections

Article: "Public Data for Gulf of America: well interventions", The Deckhand Chronicles, revision 6 of 19 Nov 2026. This page holds in full what Appendix D of the article summarises.

- **Well life.** Of 1,235 development wells first drilled in 2005–2025, 731 (59%) had at least one heavy intervention approved by 13 November 2026. The median age of all 751 development wells in that cohort still standing on that date was 14.4 years. Of the 1,655 exploration wells first drilled in the same years, 1,127 were permanently abandoned by that date, including 589 within a year of first spud. [Stage counts](https://api.github.com/repositories/574746314/contents/examples/bsee/interventions_by_depth/output/life_cycle_stage_reach.csv?ref=78f1a9183a75e56adb7f31e88b271864f0b69d37) and [well life](https://api.github.com/repositories/574746314/contents/examples/bsee/interventions_by_depth/output/life_cycle_life_years.csv?ref=78f1a9183a75e56adb7f31e88b271864f0b69d37).
- **Approvals by depth.** In 2025, 316 well-work approvals were in water under 100 m, 99 in 100 to under 500 m, 185 in 500 to under 1,500 m and 165 in 1,500 m or deeper. [Annual approvals and opening stocks](https://api.github.com/repositories/574746314/contents/examples/bsee/interventions_by_depth/output/rates.csv?ref=78f1a9183a75e56adb7f31e88b271864f0b69d37).
- **Counts and rates.** In 2006, 2,099 well-work approvals against 16,097 wells standing on 1 January gave 13.0 approvals per 100 standing wells. In 2025, 765 approvals against 7,636 standing wells gave 10.0 per 100. [Annual rates](https://api.github.com/repositories/574746314/contents/examples/bsee/interventions_by_depth/output/rates.csv?ref=78f1a9183a75e56adb7f31e88b271864f0b69d37).
- **Light and heavy work.** In each full year of 2014–2025, at least three quarters of well-work approvals named a classifiable unit (82% in 2025). Light work ranged from 5.4 to 8.5 approvals per 100 standing wells per year, and heavy work from 2.5 to 4.8. Light and heavy rates averaged 7.6 and 3.8 in 2014–2019, and 5.6 and 2.8 in 2023–2025. [Annual approvals by unit class](https://api.github.com/repositories/574746314/contents/examples/bsee/interventions_by_depth/output/intervention_weight_by_year.csv?ref=78f1a9183a75e56adb7f31e88b271864f0b69d37).
- **Estimated duration.** In 2025, the median operator estimate for well work was 14 days among 136 valid estimates in water 1,500 m or deeper and 3 days among 305 valid estimates under 100 m. The permits record estimated durations. [Permit duration estimates](https://api.github.com/repositories/574746314/contents/examples/bsee/interventions_by_depth/output/permit_duration_estimates.csv?ref=78f1a9183a75e56adb7f31e88b271864f0b69d37).
