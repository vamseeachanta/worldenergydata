# Counting rules

Article: "Public Data for Gulf of America: well interventions", The Deckhand Chronicles, revision 6 of 19 Nov 2026. This page holds in full what Appendix A of the article summarises.

1. **Wells and life.** One well is one API10 number in BSEE's borehole file, region G, with cancelled bores removed. A well starts at the earliest spud date of its bores. It ends when every bore that is not superseded (status ST or BP) is permanently abandoned (PA) with a date; the end is the latest of those dates. Otherwise it stands at 13 November 2026. Development (D) and exploration (E) follow the well type of the original hole (suffix ST00BP00).
2. **Drilling.** Each bore with a spud date: the original hole and each sidetrack or bypass. Permits that prepare new hole (PLUGBACK, sidetrack, bypass or deepen) are excluded from the permit counts so that drilling is not counted twice.
3. **Permits.** BSEE's permit table (eWellAPM) has no separate approval-status field. Every row is taken as an approved permit, and its ACC_STATUS_DATE (the Approved Date column of BSEE's permit query) as the approval date; a row without a valid date is not counted. One approved permit to modify (one submission ID) is one event. WORKOVER, ENHANCE, COMP with ZONE, OTHER, INFORM and UTIL are interventions (well work). ABANDON with PERMABAN is permanent abandonment, else with TEMPABAN temporary abandonment, else of unstated type. COMP without ZONE (initial completion) is excluded. Approvals from 2005 to 13 November 2026 are counted; the feed begins part-way through 2004.
4. **Light or heavy.** From the unit type the permit names (rig ID joined to the regulator's rig lookup). Light: wireline (WL), coiled tubing (CT), intervention vessel (WI), liftboat (LB), recorded non-rig operation (NR). Heavy: hydraulic workover or snubbing unit (HW, SB), platform rig (PF), jack-up rig (JU), drillship or semisubmersible (DS, SS, SD). Not classified: no unit named, or another type (barge, submersible, unmatched). These groups keep the unit categories of the permit counts, except that the combined "liftboat / jack-up" category is divided by its two source codes. See the [published unit mapping](https://api.github.com/repositories/574746314/contents/examples/bsee/interventions_by_depth/output/intervention_weight_mapping.csv?ref=78f1a9183a75e56adb7f31e88b271864f0b69d37).
5. **Water depth.** Multiply feet by 0.3048 to obtain metres. The bands are 0 to under 100 m, 100 to under 500 m, 500 to under 1,500 m, and 1,500 m or deeper. Each boundary enters the deeper band. Event depth uses a unique finite, nonnegative depth for the API12 bore, then a unique API10 fallback. Missing or unresolved depth stays in the “no depth” group. Inventory depth uses the canonical original hole, with a unique valid depth across remaining bores as fallback. To select a well cohort by depth, use inventory depth to choose API10s and keep all their eligible bores and permits when calculating life, approvals and opening stock. See “Method” in the [pinned study README](https://api.github.com/repositories/574746314/contents/examples/bsee/interventions_by_depth/README.md?ref=24744b528298f26c51bfce4cb3b66fa4f676eb02).
6. **Standing wells** on 1 January use the same start and end rule as item 1.
