# Cost method

Article: "Public Data for Gulf of America: well interventions", The Deckhand Chronicles, revision 6 of 19 Nov 2026. This page holds in full what Appendix C of the article summarises. The sources are listed in [README.md](README.md).

Our spread scenarios multiply a published day rate by low, base and high durations. The rigless platform spread is USD 64,530 a day, derived from a TAP-738 P&A spread (Table 8-2: USD 225,856 over 84 hours) on p. 8-12; casing jacks USD 68,424 a day (Table 8-3, p. 8-14); a drillship or semisubmersible USD 674,600 a day (p. 8-18). Rig mobilisation is excluded from the drillship or semisubmersible rows (TAP-738, p. 8-18), not assumed free; mobilisation is added only for the platform spreads: the rigless case, from TAP-738 Table 8-2, and the casing-jack case, which borrows it. Well-work durations are 7, 10.5 and 14 days (BOEM 2016-018, p. 3-37: a Gulf median of 7 days, within a range of 1 day to several months; one to two weeks from the BOEM Alaska update, 2012, p. 108). On these inputs a light well-work job on a shelf platform costs USD 0.53 to 0.98 million and a subsea job from a drillship or semisubmersible USD 4.7 to 9.4 million. The deepwater dry-tree abandonment row is TAP-738's published per-well total (Table 8-4, p. 8-15). Costs are 2015 dollars, not escalated, and are scenarios, not bids. The full table, every input and its source page are in the [full study](https://github.com/vamseeachanta/worldenergydata/tree/24744b528298f26c51bfce4cb3b66fa4f676eb02/examples/bsee/interventions_by_depth) ([cost_table.csv](https://github.com/vamseeachanta/worldenergydata/blob/78f1a9183a75e56adb7f31e88b271864f0b69d37/examples/bsee/interventions_by_depth/output/cost_table.csv)).
