The snapshot is therefore suitable for schema inspection only; field production and non-placeholder economics are not established.

Reader contract

inspect_gbb_csv(raw, max_bytes=..., max_rows=...) accepts bytes already obtained by the caller. Both resource budgets are required positive integers, chosen by that caller; they are not engineering acceptance limits. UTF-8 with an optional byte-order mark and reordered known columns is supported. Unknown, missing or duplicate columns fail. Malformed rows, invalid dates, non-finite amounts, duplicate date/facility/location keys and exceeded budgets fail without partial output. Duplicate revisions require explicit upstream resolution; no last-row-wins rule is assumed.

The publisher header is the schema basis: GasDate, FacilityName, FacilityId, FacilityType, Demand, Supply, TransferIn, TransferOut, HeldInStorage, CushionGasStorage, State, LocationName, LocationId and LastUpdated. Identifiers remain text, timestamps receive no inferred time zone and finite amounts retain their decimal values. Blank amounts are unknown, distinct from zero. Negative source values are retained without asserting physical validity. All facility types are preserved. Neither summing locations nor treating supply as field production is justified by this reader.

Verification and later slices

Synthetic tests independently check literal token readings, schema rejection, resource budgets, record identity and explicit qualification gaps. No production estimate, conversion coefficient or economic equation is introduced. The exact source-byte digest provides snapshot traceability, not an engineering-validity claim. The existing screening adapter remains the public chain entry point.

Later slices will qualify the source, evaluate PEPS-SA separately, and connect a reviewed production feed to the adapter and economics. Those slices will require sourced conversion and allocation equations, complete-period coverage checks, and independent worked cashflow examples. PEPS-SA access, schema and rights are not evaluated by this slice. The shared source catalogs were absent from the inspected metadata location; no catalog identity or production-readiness entry is asserted.
