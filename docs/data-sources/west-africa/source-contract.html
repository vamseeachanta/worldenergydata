What the sources establish

NUPRC. The client’s report index redirected to a missing page. The current archive displayed a loading message. A later capture requires a publisher-linked report, its version, rights covering the intended use and a table that identifies period and units. A blend can receive production from multiple fields. Preserve the reported stream, product and period. A rate in thousand barrels per day cannot be ingested as a monthly volume in barrels.

EITI. Both the open-data catalogue and the Nigeria page returned HTTP 403. The client’s countries, production and payments paths under https://data.eiti.org/api/v1/ are candidates. Documentation is needed to confirm filters, schema, coverage, units and rights. Preserve annual production by country, commodity and disclosed company separately from payments by country, company, payment type and currency. A reporting year gives neither a publication date nor a refresh interval. Publication lag remains unknown until both the period end and the release date are available.

Other Nigerian leads. The regulator describes well approvals, resource estimation and concession records. Downloadable well, reserves and block datasets remain unqualified. The national transparency website could not be verified over TLS. For these discovery gaps the release format, public record granularity, units, cadence and rights remain unknown. The register gives a publisher lead and release condition for each.

Storage and provenance

Resolve west_africa/nigeria/ below the configured canonical data root. Key each capture by stable source ID, reporting period and source version. Preserve revisions for the same period. Reuse a retained capture only after its digest matches.

raw/
Retain original bytes only after the source-specific terms permit acquisition and retention. Record redistribution rights separately before publication.
extracted/
Extract PDF text and tables first. Keep page, table, heading and unit locators. Inspect only the relevant pages when layout is ambiguous.
normalized/
Keep source keys, grain, observation period and original units. Link each row to the corresponding raw digest and table or API record.
manifests/
Record stable source ID, source URL, reporting period, release date, source version, UTC retrieval time, byte count, SHA-256 of the original bytes, rights evidence and allowed uses. Record the declared update cadence separately. Use an explicit unknown and reason for missing metadata.
quality/
Record missing periods, revisions, schema or unit conflicts, publication lag and rejected records. An access failure leaves coverage unknown. A reported zero requires an identified observation.

Conditions for field analysis

The country adapter checklist governs the field chain under issue #713. A verified field identifier, monthly production by fluid in declared units, and dated field metadata must be available for the same field. Assigning a blend total to a dominant field or spreading annual data over months would manufacture field history.

Once those inputs qualify, map production through AbstractProductionAdapter and to_fdas_production, and map field metadata through FieldMetaMapping to FieldConcept. Validate the regional fixture against the existing conformance suite. Future scheduler registration must satisfy the source-refresh acceptance criteria, including independently verified source vintage and last successful refresh.
