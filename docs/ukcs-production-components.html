UKCS production-component extraction

The UKCS loader extracts separate condensate volume and operational streams from source-supported PPRS fields. Missing measurements remain NaN. The unified adapter retains its existing columns and passes condensate through; FDAS receives its existing oil, gas and water schema.

Method and completeness

Conversion factors are imported from worldenergydata.common.units. NIST SP 811, Appendix B.8 (web edition 2016) lists the petroleum barrel and cubic foot in cubic metres. Their inverses support the shared rounded factors used here:

liquid_bbl = reported_m3 * 6.2898
gas_mcf = reported_ksm3 * 35.3147
gas_mcf_total = associated_gas_mcf + dry_gas_mcf

The thousand prefixes cancel in the gas conversion. Reference conditions are inherited from the source; this conversion does not apply a pressure or temperature correction. Condensate is never added to oil, and injection, flaring and venting are never added to produced gas. No NGL total is inferred from condensate. An explicit source zero remains zero; an absent column, null, non-numeric value or infinity becomes unknown. Both gas components are required for a total.

Annual aggregation sums observed records per field and year only when every observed value for that component is known. A missing value makes that component's annual total unknown. Absent calendar months, duplicate records and reporting-unit overlap are not reconciled by this operation. A sum of observed records does not establish a complete calendar-year total.

Worked example

Run python examples/ukcs/production_components.py with the repository packages importable. Inputs are synthetic: January condensate is 10 m3; February condensate is null. The independent hand calculation is 10 * 6.2898 = 62.898 bbl, displayed as 62.9 bbl. February and the observed-month annual condensate total remain unknown. NGL remains unknown for both months. No measured production is supplied by this example.

The fixture tests/fixtures/ukcs/pprs_components.json exercises the inspected field names using synthetic values. Tests independently calculate the expected conversions and cover explicit zeros, absent fields, nulls, non-mapping cases, annual incompleteness and the adapter-to-FDAS bridge.

Compatibility and limits

The loader adds component columns; consumers selecting by column name retain the existing fields. Empty frames carry the extended schema. Older normalized loaders without condensate remain supported by the adapter, with unknown condensate output. STANDARD_COLUMNS is unchanged; operational streams and NGL stay on the UKCS loader surface. FDAS has no condensate input, so separate condensate revenue is not established by this change.

The existing crude-oil mass factor and legacy thousand-tonne oil/water conversions are retained for compatibility. These are approximate and do not establish a source-specific density. PPRS oil-volume, density-based condensate, separate NGL and daily-rate fallback calculations are not evaluated. A stream without a defensible extracted volume remains unknown rather than zero.
