Country wells and drilling inputs

FDAS accepts source-scoped well records and explicitly supplied active drilling intervals. The DataVic adapter supplies Australian well metadata from complete local WFS GeoJSON exports. Spud and rig-release dates alone do not establish drilling activity; that case is NOT_EVALUATED and cannot be exported to cashflow.

Inputs and contract

WellRecord in fdas.adapters.wells requires country, source, well ID and development. Its key is the tuple (country, source, well ID). Spud is a nullable calendar date and water depth is nullable metres. Zero depth is distinct from missing depth. Foreign IDs are not US API well numbers.

read_datavic_wells(raw, development_by_site_id=..., water_depth_unit=...) in fdas.adapters.datavic_wells accepts bytes of a complete WFS FeatureCollection. Both numberMatched and numberReturned shall equal the feature count; a next-page link or duplicate ID is rejected. Completeness refers only to the query supplied by the caller, not to Australia or an entire development.

The source's siteid identifies wells. An explicit site-ID-to-development mapping is required; well names, leases and title polygons are not field links. The source record IDs and raw SHA-256 are retained in the returned DataVicWells. The caller shall retain the original export, query URL, UTC retrieval time, source vintage, licence and evidence for the field links and units.

DataVic's schema names spud_date, rig_release_date and abswaterdepth but does not establish water-depth units or datum. The adapter requires a caller-selected unit (m or international ft); None retains unknown depth as None. The international foot is exactly 0.3048 m according to the cited NIST table. Datum-dependent interpretation remains unqualified. ISO dates and datetimes preserve the source calendar day without UTC shifting.

Method and coverage

DrillingActivity in fdas.data.country_drilling requires the same source-scoped key, a start date, an exclusive end date and an evidence reference. These are active drilling intervals supplied from activity records or explicitly labelled scenario assumptions, not spans inferred from milestones. Completion, suspension and inactive gaps shall be excluded by the caller.

build_drilling_timeline(wells, activities, development=...) rejects unknown well IDs, missing spud evidence, pre-spud activity and overlapping intervals for the same well. Adjacent intervals are permitted. Concurrent wells contribute separate well-days. Every supplied well in the selected development shall have activity; otherwise the monthly result is None, the missing keys are listed and as_cashflow_input() raises ValueError. EVALUATED establishes coverage of the supplied inventory only; gaps in source activity are not detectable.

Monthly days are the count of active calendar dates in the intersection of each half-open interval with each month. Gregorian date subtraction supplies that count; no utilization or duration coefficient is applied. The evaluated timeline exports the existing drilling_monthly cashflow dictionary.

Worked example: synthetic inputs

Run examples/fdas/country_wells.py with the FDAS package available. One synthetic well is explicitly linked to Example. The supplied drilling interval is [2024-01-30, 2024-02-03), and the selected scenario daily rate is 0.5 million USD/day. Water depth is unknown and is not used in this calculation.

The dimensional equation is CAPEX = active days × daily rate, as implemented by the existing CashflowEngine.calculate_drilling_capex. Each month's answer is 2 × 0.5 = 1.0 million USD. This example calls that method directly and keeps its documented million-USD units explicit; it does not qualify the legacy monthly cashflow model's currency-labelled fields or a country's fiscal terms.

Sources and limits

  • DataVic petroleum wells catalogue: dataset ID 37083d2f-f6ea-4813-af54-ca8cd754262d, Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International; metadata modified 2026-07-30, inspected 2026-10-20.
  • DataVic WFS schema: field names and nullable types, inspected 2026-10-20. No live well records are reproduced; the example and fixtures are synthetic.
  • NIST revised unit conversion factors: 2023 adoption, international-foot row, inspected 2026-10-20.

The source observations and digests are recorded in country-wells-sources.json. The earlier combined DataVic dataset URL returns HTTP 404; the current catalogue entry above supersedes that locator for this adapter. Existing shared source catalogues were unavailable in the inspected checkout, so catalog reconciliation is not established. No downloader, scheduler job, national well inventory, measured activity series, production data or Australian fiscal deck is supplied. DataVic spud-only economics remains NOT_EVALUATED until activity evidence is supplied.
