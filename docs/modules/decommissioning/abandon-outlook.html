Field and well Abandon outlook

The Abandon stage exposes the supplied projected cessation-of-production (COP) year, an uncalibrated wells-only P&A estimate, regulatory reference topics and explicit data gaps. These outputs provide context for further engineering; neither a field budget nor regulatory compliance is established.

Inputs and method

reports/lower_tertiary/lifecycle/_facts.json supplies field water depth and projected COP. config/ong_field_development/fields_registry.yml supplies validated_economics.wellbores. The count includes recorded wellbores; it does not establish the remaining inventory requiring abandonment.

The conversion is d = water depth in feet × 0.3048 metres/foot, from NIST SP 811 (2008), Appendix B.8, foot. The repository's DecommissioningCostEstimator computes C = (base + depth rate × d) × regional multiplier for a well without a weight term. The illustrative field total is recorded wellbores × C.

The cost-factor source is packages/worldenergydata-decommissioning/src/worldenergydata/decommissioning/cost_model.py, _COST_FACTORS["well_p_and_a"] and _REGION_MULTIPLIERS["gom"]. The factors are assumed, based on previous experience. Published calibration evidence is absent: engineering cost qualification is NOT_EVALUATED. The displayed cost reference year is inherited from that model; no inflation adjustment is applied.

Worked example

A synthetic input has water depth 1,000 ft and two recorded wellbores. The converted depth is 304.8 m. Using the repository assumptions (base 8 MUSD, depth rate 0.015 MUSD/m and regional multiplier 1), the per-well estimate is displayed as 12.6 MUSD and the aggregate as 25.1 MUSD. The exact independent arithmetic is written in test_hand_calculation in tests/unit/lower_tertiary/test_decom_outlook_contract.py. The comparison establishes arithmetic agreement only, not predictive accuracy.

Reproduction

python scripts/lower_tertiary/build_decom_outlook.py
python scripts/lower_tertiary/build_lifecycle_posters.py
python scripts/lower_tertiary/build_well_timelines.py

Run from the repository root with the repository packages and their dependencies available. The generated sidecar echoes inputs, model reference year and SHA-256 digests of the facts, count registry, model and regulatory index. It is consumed by both poster and well builders and copied by the static-site builder.

Limits and engineering handoff

  • Missing or invalid depth withholds the estimate. Missing count withholds the field total; explicit zero is preserved. Missing sidecars hide the field card and leave well estimates unavailable.
  • BSEE liability bands remain null without usable source data and qualified canonical-field attribution. A populated source alone does not establish a band.
  • Regulatory topics and references are read through get_regulations("gom"); thresholds and deadlines are not copied. Current applicability and compliance require review of the governing requirements.
  • COP is a supplied projection, not a newly fitted decline or late-life forecast. P&A filing status is not inferred from production status.
  • Host removal, pipelines, subsea equipment, cost escalation and engineering qualification are excluded. The Abandon context page provides the route to P&A tubular, structural removal and marine-lift engineering in digitalmodel.
  • Poster decommissioning links resolve in the built public site; the Abandon norms link also resolves within the committed lifecycle directory.
