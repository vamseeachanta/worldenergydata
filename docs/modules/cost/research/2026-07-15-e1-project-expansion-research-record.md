## Structural findings (dataset-policy grade)

1. **Petrobras charter model**: leased FPSOs are excluded from its capex per SEC 6-Ks (lease commitments disclosed separately) — per-system pre-salt "costs" in trade press are modeled, not disclosed. Brazil sample therefore skews to IOC/independent operators.
2. **Norway-domestic operators sanction NOK-only** — every Norwegian megaproject except foreign-operated Martin Linge landed TODO; Equinor states USD only for its UK/Brazil projects. Same operator can label the same number two currencies (Nexen US$/C$ 3.3bn).
3. **Shell non-disclosure is generational and global** (Perdido, Stones, Mars B, Appomattox, Whale, Sparta, Prelude, Gumusut, Malikai, Crux, Bonga) — a sampling bias to note wherever the dataset is presented.
4. **SEC-hosted partner filings are the single richest unlock** (Chevron/BHP/Total/Statoil/Murphy/Noble filings carried 10+ figures operators' newsrooms never did); operator IR pages systematically bot-block.
5. **Basis heterogeneity is severe in the 2000s generation** — initial-phase vs full-field-through-year vs excludes-leased-FPS vs stated-at-first-oil. The `capex_basis` sentence is load-bearing.
