## Method

v1 was assembled from **already-committed, verbatim-sourced** trail points surfaced during the
E1 project expansion (#1024), the #1019 second-source validation, and the #1020 award research —
so every point re-uses a citation the project has already stood behind. Each point carries
currency, kind (sanction/revised/spend-to-date/final), provenance tier, verbatim quote, and URL.
