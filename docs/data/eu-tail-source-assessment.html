Method

The issue decision gate permits field/well production OR usable field metadata. Monthly production fit, concession mapping and reuse are assessed separately. No source is automatically enrolled in an analysis chain.

The Boolean gate is: verified field/well production OR verified usable field metadata. A concession satisfies the production branch only with a verified field mapping. Monthly FDAS fit additionally requires verified monthly resolution. These are logical criteria from the linked issue, not engineering equations; no physical constants, volume conversions, economic assumptions or numeric acceptance limits are introduced.

A PROMOTE disposition identifies a capability candidate. Adapter conformance, full-data completeness, freshness, units and lawful intended use remain prerequisites. NOT_EVALUATED exposes missing evidence; it does not mean a true zero or a country-wide absence of production. ATTRIBUTION_REQUIRED describes the stated licence condition, not a completed reuse-compliance check. No automated enrolment is performed.
