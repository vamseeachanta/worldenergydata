Monthly gas revenue input contract

The gas-pricing helper validates calendar periods and numeric quantities before monthly revenue is calculated. Invalid inputs raise ValueError; gas-validation errors encountered by the cashflow engine are reported as CashflowError.

Required inputs

  • The production frame carries YEAR_MONTH and MONTHLY_GAS_MCF, with one row per production month.
  • After the existing string conversion, every production period has the exact ASCII form YYYY-MM and identifies a calendar month representable by Python's date class. Missing periods, whitespace, full dates and unpadded months are rejected.
  • The gas deck contains an explicit realized price in USD/Mcf for every production month. Unused deck entries do not affect the calculation.
  • Prices and volumes are finite, nonnegative quantities. Boolean values, including NumPy Boolean scalars, are rejected. Numeric strings remain supported; conversion overflow is reported as a validation error.
  • Explicit zero prices and volumes remain valid. Duplicate production months and non-finite revenue products remain invalid.

Arithmetic and source basis

Monthly revenue [USD] = monthly volume [Mcf] × realized price [USD/Mcf]. The EIA Natural Gas Prices definitions, “Average Price of Deliveries to Consumers” (undated, accessed 2026-10-19), define average price through revenue and associated sales volume. This supports the arithmetic only; it supplies no Spanish market-price assumption.

Calendar validation follows the Python 3.11 datetime documentation, date constructor and date.fromisoformat (accessed 2026-10-19). The monthly key shape and nonnegative quantity domain retain the repository's gas-revenue contract at revision 648d6de4.

The regression examples are synthetic: 100 Mcf × 3 USD/Mcf = 300 USD; 200 Mcf × 4 USD/Mcf = 800 USD. Expected values are calculated independently in the test documentation. A zero multiplicand gives zero revenue.

Compatibility and limits

Validation applies when a gas deck is supplied. Omitting the deck retains legacy oil-only cashflows. Existing valid gas revenue, royalty and net cashflow arithmetic is unchanged. The Spain reference chain uses the same cashflow engine; separate revenue-total reporting is outside this change.

No market series, heating-value factor, currency conversion, gas-specific operating cost or Spain-specific fiscal assumption is introduced. Priced gas does not establish the completeness of development economics.
