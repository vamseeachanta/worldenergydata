Gulf of Mexico interventions: plan-lite

Related activity-characterisation issue: worldenergydata issue 1220. This example will extend the current task's scope to rates and indicative costs without closing the broader issue.

The analysis will count public BSEE APM approvals by calendar year and borehole water depth. It will distinguish reported completed operations from permits and weekly activity records.

Approach and steps

  1. The four confirmed raw-data links will be downloaded to the ignored dated directory. Receipts will retain URLs, UTC times, bytes, page dates and SHA-256 digests.
  2. Synthetic tests will precede the implementation. The existing API normalizer will be reused. Local counting functions will retain missing depth and reject conflicting submission identities.
  3. Primary codes and structured suboperations will define disjoint groups. New-hole work will be separated. Original-hole API10 inventory will provide an approximate year-end denominator.
  4. Public cost sources will support explicit low/base/high scenarios, with source-year dollars and cited duration assumptions. Unknown costs will remain missing.
  5. CSV aggregates, three charts and a short results note will be generated, inspected and checked for determinism. One public PR will be opened.

Checks and acceptance

Tests will cover band edges, normalization, fallback, deduplication, grouping, inventory dates, rates, cost arithmetic and full-year selection. APM/EOR 2025 totals will be reconciled to the supplied checkpoint. Archive digests will be verified before counting. All chart PNGs will be at least 1600 pixels wide.

Resource intelligence

completion_intervention/activity.py and report.py will inform archive grains and provenance. intervention/activity_by_band.py and its YAMLs use different depth bands and incomplete WAR depth. war_rig_days.py will inform inclusive interval counting; war_activity_codes.py cautions that several codes lack published definitions. data/apm_data.py loads historical pickles, so the fresh raw tables will be read directly. The shared API normalizer will be imported. Existing day-rate YAMLs contain proxies and will not be treated as verified quotes. Borehole and WAR dictionaries will be checked against actual headers. The source catalog identifies BSEE Data Center as bsee-data-center.

Risk and scope

Permit approvals will not establish performed work. Current status will not establish a permit's historical operation. EOR status dates and original-hole inventory will be approximate. Cost scenarios will not be market averages. Raw archives and extracts will remain uncommitted. No unrelated module, confidential source, escalation, deployment or merge will be included.

Review route

Execution will be single-lane as explicitly requested. Defect-focused plan and artifact checks will be performed in this session; independent provider review will not be claimed. The user instruction prohibits other models and overrides the standing cross-provider review route.
